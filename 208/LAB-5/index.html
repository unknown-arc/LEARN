EXPLORE INDIA

10 sacred destinations

Ancient stories. Remarkable architecture. Unforgettable journeys.

01 Must Explore

VARANASI, UTTAR PRADESH

Kashi Vishwanath Temple

One of the twelve Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva, standing on the western bank of the holy Ganga.

Architecture: North Indian Nagara style with a gold-plated spire.

Explore more ↗
02

AMRITSAR, PUNJAB

Golden Temple

Harmandir Sahib sits within the sacred Amrit Sarovar and is renowned for its golden beauty and Langar.

Experience: Sikh architecture and a tradition of free community meals.

Explore more ↗
03

MADURAI, TAMIL NADU

Meenakshi Amman Temple

A spectacular Dravidian temple known for towering gopurams and thousands of sculptures.

Highlight: The famous Hall of a Thousand Pillars.

Explore more ↗
04

TIRUMALA, ANDHRA PRADESH

Tirupati Balaji Temple

Dedicated to Lord Venkateswara and set among the seven hills of Tirumala.

Tradition: Devotees offer their hair as a mark of devotion.

Explore more ↗
05

PURI, ODISHA

Jagannath Temple

One of India's four Char Dham pilgrimage sites, celebrated for its grand Rath Yatra.

Architecture: Kalinga style with a distinctive curved tower.

Explore more ↗
06

PRABHAS PATAN, GUJARAT

Somnath Temple

A Jyotirlinga temple on the Arabian Sea coastline with a long history of rebuilding.

Style: Chaulukya-inspired architecture.

Explore more ↗
07

NEW DELHI, DELHI

Akshardham Temple

A modern monument showcasing traditional Hindu architectural craftsmanship.

Highlight: Pink sandstone, white marble and evening fountain shows.

Explore more ↗
08

KONARK, ODISHA

Konark Sun Temple

A UNESCO World Heritage Site designed as a giant stone chariot dedicated to Surya.

Wonder: Intricately carved wheels can function as sundials.

Explore more ↗
09

KATRA, JAMMU & KASHMIR

Vaishno Devi Temple

A cave shrine in the Trikuta Mountains reached by a pilgrimage route from Katra.

Journey: The shrine is around 5,200 feet above sea level.

Explore more ↗
10

KEDARNATH, UTTARAKHAND

Kedarnath Temple

A Himalayan Jyotirlinga surrounded by snow-capped peaks and reached through a pilgrimage journey.

Altitude: Approximately 3,580 metres above sea level.

Explore more ↗

Plan before you go

01

Best time to visit

02

Transportation

Choose from airports, railway stations, local taxis, buses and pilgrimage routes.

03

Accommodation

04

Visitor guidelines

Dress respectfully and follow each temple's entry and visitor rules.

05

Photography
